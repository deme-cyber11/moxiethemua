About Jessica

With over 8 years of experience in bridal and editorial makeup, Jessica brings a blend of classic elegance and modern artistry to every client.

Jessica's approach centers on enhancing your natural beauty while creating a look that's uniquely you. She takes the time to understand each client's vision, skin type, and personal style to deliver results that feel both elevated and authentic.

Whether you're walking down the aisle or stepping in front of the camera, Jessica ensures your look is flawless, long-lasting, and photograph-ready. Her calm, warm demeanor puts even the most nervous brides at ease.
