The MoxietheMua Story

Bringing premium bridal artistry to every corner of Texas.

Our Story

Born from a Passion for Bridal Beauty

MoxietheMua was founded with a simple belief: every bride deserves access to exceptional makeup artistry, no matter where in Texas she's getting married.

What started as a single artist's passion for bridal beauty has grown into a network of elite, vetted professionals spanning cities across the Lone Star State. We saw that talented artists were working in isolation and brides were struggling to find trusted professionals — so we built the bridge between them.

Our Mission: To connect every bride with a skilled, vetted artist who can bring her vision to life — with the consistency and quality of an established brand.

Our Vision: To become the most trusted name in bridal beauty across Texas and beyond.

Meet the Founder

Amari Robinson

Amari Robinson is the creative force behind MoxietheMua. A licensed makeup artist and Austin native, Amari spent years perfecting her craft in the bridal beauty industry before realizing that the biggest challenge wasn't talent — it was access.

"I kept meeting incredible artists who were struggling to build a client base, while brides were scrolling endlessly trying to find someone they could trust. I knew there had to be a better way."

In founding MoxietheMua, Amari set out to build the network she wished existed when she started — one that empowers independent artists with brand support, lead generation, and a professional community, while giving brides a reliable destination for vetted, top-tier talent.

When she's not building the MoxietheMua brand, Amari can be found mentoring emerging artists, staying current on beauty trends, and working with brides one-on-one in her hometown of Austin, TX.
